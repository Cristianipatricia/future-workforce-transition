# Future Workforce Transition

## Overview

AI and analytics solution focused on workforce reskilling, digital inclusion, burnout prevention, and workforce adaptation in the era of automation and artificial intelligence.

The project was developed to address the growing impact of automation on vulnerable workers and support organizations in building more resilient and future-ready teams.

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of workers face increasing risks of job displacement due to automation and rapid AI adoption. At the same time, companies struggle with workforce reskilling, burnout, and digital inclusion challenges.

The project explores how artificial intelligence, analytics, and personalized learning paths can support workforce transition and reduce technological exclusion.
